M&E Design for Safety Ontology

An ontology for representing Design for Safety (DfS) knowledge associated with mechanical and electrical systems in construction.

Explore interactive ontology Download OWL ontology

Purpose

The ontology represents concepts and relationships used to support knowledge-based Design for Safety analysis of M&E systems, activities, hazards, controls, stakeholders and related concepts.

Interactive visualization

Use the WebVOWL viewer to explore classes and relationships. You can zoom, pan, search and inspect individual ontology elements.
